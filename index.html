Frequently Asked Questions

Know before you begin.

Does Odyssey replace school track practice?

No. Odyssey is designed to supplement an athlete’s school program, school-issued workouts, and independent training responsibilities.

Who is Odyssey training designed for?

Odyssey typically works with athletes at the high-school level and beyond. Junior and adult athletes share one performance-focused training environment while the coaching is individualized around their intentions.

Where can I find current training plans and schedule details?

The Athlete Training page contains the currently published plans, recurring-day structure, schedule, and location.

Do junior and adult athletes use separate programs?

No. Odyssey uses one performance system. Athletes may train together while their coaching, responsibilities, and goals reflect their individual intentions.

What happens after I submit the Interest Form?

Odyssey is notified when the form is submitted. We will email you to arrange an introductory call about your goals, program details, and pricing. Submitting the form expresses interest; it does not confirm enrollment, a training plan, selected training days, or a separate coaching consultation.
